My Workout

Monday · Wednesday · Friday — same every week · 30–35 min
Rest 2 min after 1–3 · Do 4 + 5 back to back · Abs last

1Incline dumbbell press3 × 6–10
2Machine chest press3 × 8–12
3Seated dumbbell overhead press3 × 8–12 Stop just short of shrugging at the top.

2Seated cable row3 × 8–12 Pull to your belly button, elbows close to your sides.

4Rear-delt fly machine2 × 15–20 Elbows at shoulder height. Never above.

6Hanging or lying leg raise ABS3 × 10–15 Slow, no swinging. Curl your hips up at the top — that is the rep.

1Leg press3 × 8–15 Deep, knees to about 90°. First 3 weeks: 2 sets only.

3Dumbbell lateral raise3 × 12–20 Second dose on purpose — shoulder width is your fastest win.

5Overhead triceps extension3 × 10–15

No time or no energy? Do only the two shaded exercises and leave — 12 minutes, and it still counts. Never skip the whole day.

Why 17% showed no abs

17% is not six-pack territory — abs start showing around 12–15%, a real six-pack near 10–12%. And scales and calipers read 5–8 points low, so 17% was probably low 20s. You were never at 17%, and you had no ab muscle to reveal.

How hard — the big one

Last set of every exercise: go until you cannot get another clean rep. Earlier sets, stop 1–2 short. Short sessions only work if the last set is genuinely hard.

Adding weight

Hit the top rep number on every set? Go up next week. +5 lb per dumbbell · +10 lb machines. 6–12 reps on big lifts, 12–20 on small ones.

Your arms — uneven and growing

Every dumbbell set: weaker arm first, then match its exact rep count with the strong one. Never do extra. A dominant arm is normal and it evens out over months.

Stretch is where growth is

3 seconds lowering, full stretch at the bottom, no bouncing. It is also why this list picks overhead extensions, incline curls and the seated leg curl.

Every day

Protein 1 g per lb of goal weight. Creatine 5 g. Salt your food. Sleep 7–9 h. Losing slowly is what protects the 450 to 730 and your arms.
